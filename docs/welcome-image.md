# Image de bienvenue MODEXA

Image créée avec l’outil intégré de génération d’images, puis encodée en JPEG pour le chargement mobile. Fichier utilisé : `dist/assets/welcome-fashion.jpg` (environ 249 Ko). Le nom MODEXA et les boutons sont du texte HTML accessible, séparé de l’image.

## Prompt de génération

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: full-screen portrait background for MODEXA, a French mobile clothing catalogue app for fashion boutiques. Create a premium photographic collage on a pure black background, aspect ratio 9:19.5. Ten large separated slightly rotated rectangular fashion editorial photographs, partly cropped at the edges, arranged like floating cards down the tall canvas. Subjects: beautifully tailored West African wax-print dresses on adult Black female fashion models, elegant men's embroidered tunics on adult Black male models, a cream suit, sculptural orange dress, rich indigo fabric detail, colourful folded wax fabrics, a sewing atelier detail. Contemporary stylish, real photographic textiles, sophisticated amber orange, indigo, cream, green and burgundy. Large black gaps between the cards, particularly in upper centre at 18 percent height for a white MODEXA wordmark to be added in HTML. The lower 24 percent must be quiet and dark enough for two full-width buttons to be added in HTML. Similar composition to a dark mobile welcome screen with a tilted collage, crisp images, no gradients baked over whole canvas: the app will add a dark overlay. Do not create any UI, buttons, lettering, brand logos, watermarks, or captions. Portrait edge-to-edge artwork only.
